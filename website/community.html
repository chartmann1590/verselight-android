Community Guidelines

LumaVerse welcomes sincere questions, encouragement, testimony, and respectful disagreement. Speak to others as people of dignity. Join on Google Play

Not allowed

Threats, targeted harassment, hateful or dehumanizing content, sexual exploitation, strong profanity, spam, impersonation, doxxing, and encouragement of self-harm are prohibited. Do not use comments to solicit money or publish private contact details.

Moderation

Drafts receive an on-device safety check. Community members can report posts. Three distinct reports temporarily hide a comment pending review; moderators may restore or remove it. False or coordinated reporting may result in account restrictions.

Reporting immediate danger

LumaVerse is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the United States and Canada, call or text 988 for crisis support.
