Privacy Policy

VerseLight is designed so your email, likes, share history, reminder preference, and private activity history are not visible to other users.

Information we collect

If you create an account, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, Google account identifier when applicable, display name, avatar, and authentication metadata. VerseLight stores your chosen public display name and avatar, private likes and activity history, public comments, reports you submit, and basic operational timestamps.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate you, synchronize your private journey, show your public comments, prevent abuse, process reports, operate reminders, secure the service, and delete data at your request. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Public information

Comments, your chosen display name, and avatar are public. Your email, likes, share history, reporter identity, and authentication provider are not public. Do not include sensitive personal information in a comment.

On-device safety

Draft comments are evaluated locally before posting. The draft is not sent to a moderation API. Only comments you choose to post are sent to Firebase.

On-device translation

If you select a language other than English, Google ML Kit downloads a translation model to your device. Interface text, Scripture, and community comments are translated locally and are not sent to a cloud translation service. Machine translations may not always be accurate. Your language choice is stored on your device.

Diagnostics and performance

Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring process crash reports, app stability information, device and operating-system details, app version, performance traces, and network timing information. We use this technical information to fix crashes and improve responsiveness; it is not used for advertising.

Service providers

Google Firebase provides authentication, database services, crash reporting, performance monitoring, and public website hosting. Google ML Kit provides downloadable on-device language models. Cloudflare processes posted comments and abuse reports and operates the moderation queue. These providers may process technical data according to their respective privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Account deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account, profile, private activity, likes, and authored comments. A request can also be submitted from the public account deletion page without reinstalling the app.

You can delete your account from Profile → Delete my account and private data. VerseLight removes your profile, private activity, and authored comments. Security and moderation audit records may be retained when necessary to prevent abuse or comply with law, without being displayed publicly.

Security and children

We use access controls, token verification, rate limits, and least-privilege credentials. No system is perfectly secure. VerseLight is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date. For privacy questions or deletion help, use the instructions on the support page.
