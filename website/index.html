Questions, answered

Is LumaVerse free? Does it have ads?

Yes — completely free. It contains banner & interstitial ads via Google AdMob. Personalized ads require your consent.

What Bible translation?

World English Bible (WEB) — public domain. Original reference preserved even when translated.

Does it work offline & in my language?

Yes. Daily verse works offline. 60+ languages via on-device ML Kit (~30MB, no cloud).

How do I delete my account?

In app: Profile → Delete my account. Or on the web at delete-account.html — completed within 30 days.
