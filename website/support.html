Support & account deletion

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open VerseLight and select Profile.
  2. Choose Delete my account and private data.
  3. Confirm Delete permanently.

This removes your Firebase profile, private likes and activity, and comments you authored. You may be asked to sign in again if Firebase requires recent authentication.

Request deletion without the app

Open a private support request by emailing the address shown on the VerseLight Google Play listing from the email used for your account. We will verify ownership before deletion. Never post account information in a public GitHub issue.

Troubleshooting

For sign-in problems, verify that Google Play services is current, check your connection, and try again. Password users can select “Reset password” in the sign-in dialog. For notification issues, allow notifications in Android Settings and remove battery restrictions for VerseLight.
